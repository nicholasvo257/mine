Level 1 Rocket

About

Designed, built, launched, and recovered L1 Rocket with UCI Rocket Project Solids and received my certification with the National Association of Rocketry.

License: National Association of Rocketry Level 1 Rocket
License Number: NAR #124746
Date Achieved: 11/22/2025
Renewal Date: 11/20/2026

This project involved everything from initial design using OpenRocket to selecting the correct black powder motor for a stable and controlled flight. I led the manufacturing and assembly of the fiberglass and carbon fiber airframe components.

Project Details

The final certification was achieved in challenging weather conditions at the Lucerne Valley launch site. The rocket performed flawlessly, reaching an apogee of 2,100 feet and deploying the parachute for a successful recovery.

  • Materials: Fiberglass nose cone and airframe, carbon fiber motor mount, balsa fins.
  • Design Tool: OpenRocket
  • Motor: Cesaroni J350
  • Apogee: ~2,100 ft
